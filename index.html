I'm a senior at Ewha Womans University studying Computer Science with a minor in Artificial Intelligence, interested in edge ML and on-device inference, model compression (pruning & quantization), and reinforcement learning. I'm currently an undergraduate research assistant at the AI Computing Platform Lab (ACPL), where I work on the power efficiency of large language models running directly on mobile devices, and previously did research at the Multiagent Communications and Networking Lab (MCNL) on pruning and quantization for edge deployment. I also lead HoPy, a student-run practical machine learning club.
